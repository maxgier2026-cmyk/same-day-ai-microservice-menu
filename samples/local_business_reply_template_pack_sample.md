# Sample Deliverable: Local Business Reply Template Pack

Scenario: a small home-service business receives appointment and quote requests through Facebook Messenger and email. This sample uses fictional business details and is provided only to show deliverable format. A real buyer must provide their real business facts, service area, pricing rules, and policies.

## Intake summary

- Business type: home-service provider
- Channel: Facebook Messenger / email
- Bottleneck: repetitive replies for quotes, scheduling, rescheduling, and FAQs
- Compliance boundary: no fake reviews, no promises of guaranteed availability, no collection of sensitive payment information in chat

## Template 1 — New quote request

Hi [name], thanks for reaching out. I can help with a quote for [service].

To price it accurately, please send:
1. Your general location / ZIP code
2. A brief description of the job
3. 2-4 clear photos if relevant
4. Your preferred timing

Once I have those details, I can confirm whether this is in our service area and give the next step.

## Template 2 — Appointment booking

Thanks, [name]. We can offer these appointment windows:

- [Option 1]
- [Option 2]
- [Option 3]

Please reply with the best option and your service address. We will confirm before adding it to the schedule.

## Template 3 — Out-of-service-area response

Thanks for checking with us. Unfortunately, [location] is outside our current service area, so I do not want to overpromise.

If our coverage changes, I can let you know. I appreciate you reaching out.

## Template 4 — Price range explanation

Based on what you described, jobs like this usually range from [low] to [high], depending on [main pricing factors].

That is not a final quote yet. To confirm, I need [missing detail/photo/measurement].

## Template 5 — Rescheduling

Hi [name], we need to adjust the appointment for [reason stated plainly]. I apologize for the inconvenience.

The next available options are:
- [Option 1]
- [Option 2]
- [Option 3]

Please let me know which works best, and I will confirm it right away.

## Template 6 — Review request after completed work

Hi [name], thank you for choosing us for [service]. If everything looks good, a short honest review would help other local customers find us.

Review link: [link]

No pressure — we appreciate your business either way.

## Implementation checklist

- Save approved templates in the business inbox notes or text-expander tool.
- Keep pricing placeholders updated.
- Do not ask staff to copy/paste without reading the customer's actual message.
- Review templates monthly for policy or service-area changes.
