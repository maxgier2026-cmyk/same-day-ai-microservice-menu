# Marketplace Listing Cleanup — Before/After Sample

Status: Public sample deliverable for Venture 02. This demonstrates the $50 Marketplace Listing Cleanup service using a fictional generic item so no seller, buyer, platform, or product condition claim is misrepresented.

AI disclosure: Drafted with AI assistance. Real client listings must use the seller's actual item facts, photos, condition, shipping terms, and platform rules.

## Buyer-provided facts needed for a real job

| Input | Required? | Notes |

## Before listing

**Title:** Old game console

**Description:**
> Selling old game console. Works good. Comes with cords and a controller. Message me.

**Observed problems:**

- Too generic for search.
- No model/platform keywords.
- No clear condition details.
- No accessory list.
- No pickup/shipping terms.
- No buyer FAQ or safety boundaries.

## After title options

1. **Retro Game Console Bundle — Tested, 1 Controller, Power + AV Cables**
2. **[Brand/Model] Console Bundle with Controller and Cables — Tested Working**
3. **Classic Video Game Console Starter Bundle — Console, Controller, Power Cable**

Use the exact brand/model only after confirming it from the seller's item.

## After description

> Selling a tested [brand/model] game console bundle. The console powers on and loads games during a basic test. Included: console, one controller, power cable, and AV/HDMI cable as shown in the photos.
>
> Condition: pre-owned with normal cosmetic wear. Please review photos for the exact condition. Known issues: [seller fills in real issues, or "none known after basic test" if accurate].
>
> Great for someone rebuilding a retro setup or replacing a missing console. Local pickup in [area] / shipping available if the platform supports it. Cash/payment handled through the platform's approved method only.
>
> Questions welcome. I can confirm included cables/accessories before pickup/shipping.

## Photo checklist

- Front of console
- Back/ports
- Controller close-up
- Cables/accessories grouped together
- Console powered on, if possible
- Model/serial label if safe to show under platform rules
- Close-up of scratches, cracks, discoloration, or missing covers

## Pricing note template

> Suggested list price range: Check 5-10 recently sold comparable listings for the exact model and condition. Start near the middle of sold comps if the item is tested with cables; price lower if untested, missing cables, or cosmetically damaged. Seller makes final pricing decision.

## Buyer reply scripts

**Is it available?**
> Yes, it is available. Included items are: [list]. Pickup/shipping is [terms].

**Does it work?**
> It was tested by [specific test performed]. Known issues: [real issue list]. Please review photos before buying.

**Will you take less?**
> I can consider reasonable offers. The listed price is based on comparable sold listings for the same model/condition.

**Can you hold it?**
> I can hold it until [time] once pickup/payment terms are confirmed through the platform-approved method.

## Compliance boundaries

- Do not claim "new," "mint," "rare," or "fully tested" unless true.
- Do not route around platform payment/safety rules.
- Do not hide defects.
- Do not use fake urgency or fake buyer interest.
