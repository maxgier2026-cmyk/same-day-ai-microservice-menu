## AI Workflow: Customer Email Response Assistant

### Overview

You receive 10-30 customer emails per day asking about order status, return policy, product availability, and basic troubleshooting. You want AI to draft responses so you can review and send faster — not auto-reply, just draft faster.

### Step 1: Set up your prompt template

Save this as a note or document you can paste into any AI tool (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.):

```
You are helping me draft kind, clear, and accurate customer service emails for my small business, [Business Name]. I sell [brief description of what you sell].

Rules:
- Always be kind and assume good intent.
- Never make up policies. If you don't know something, flag it with [NEEDS MY REVIEW].
- Keep responses under 5 sentences unless the customer needs detailed instructions.
- Do not promise refunds or replacements unless I explicitly say to.
- Always include a clear next step for the customer.

I will paste the customer's email below. Draft a response I can edit and send.

Customer email:
---
[paste customer email here]
---
```

### Step 2: Use it for common scenarios

| Customer asks | Prompt addition | Time saved per email |
|---|---|---|
| "Where is my order?" | Add: "The tracking number is [TRACKING]. The last update was [STATUS] on [DATE]." | ~3 minutes |
| "What's your return policy?" | Add: "Our return policy is [copy policy text]. They ordered on [DATE], and the item is [item]." | ~4 minutes |
| "Do you have [product] in stock?" | Add: "Current inventory for [product]: [in stock / out of stock / pre-order]. Alternatives: [list 1-2]." | ~3 minutes |
| "This doesn't work — help" | Add: "Troubleshooting steps: 1) [step], 2) [step], 3) [step]. If those don't work, I'll escalate." | ~5 minutes |

### Step 3: Daily workflow

1. **Morning batch (5 min):** Open your email. For each customer email, copy their message, paste it into your AI tool with the prompt template + any scenario-specific addition. Review the AI draft. Edit if needed. Send.
2. **Afternoon batch (5 min):** Repeat for any new emails.
3. **End of day (2 min):** Save any [NEEDS MY REVIEW] items to handle the next morning.

### Step 4: Prompt improvements over time

After one week, review what worked:
- Which drafts needed no edits? (The prompt is good for those.)
- Which drafts needed heavy edits? (Add a new rule to the prompt.)
- Any patterns? (Create a new scenario addition.)

### Checklist for consistent results

- [ ] Prompt template saved and easily accessible
- [ ] Business name, policies, and common scenarios added to template
- [ ] Tested with 3 real customer emails
- [ ] Scenario additions created for top 5 question types
- [ ] Daily workflow time blocked (morning + afternoon, 5 min each)
