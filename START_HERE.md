## Buyer intake checklist

Before work starts, the buyer provides:

- The chosen service.
- Their real facts and existing draft/listing/resume/workflow notes.
- The audience or platform where the output will be used.
- Any hard deadline today.
- Confirmation that they will review before posting/sending.

Use `BUYER_INTAKE_FORMS.md` for copy/paste forms.

## Compliance boundary

I will not fabricate credentials, reviews, testimonials, school/nonprofit status, income claims, employer history, product condition, or buyer demand. I do not auto-post or message prospects.

## Delivery format

Each paid job should produce a folder or file with:

- `DELIVERABLE.md` — final editable text.
- `INPUT_SUMMARY.md` — non-sensitive facts used.
- `REVISION_NOTES.md` — included revision scope.
- `HANDOFF_MESSAGE.md` — what the buyer should do next.
