📝 How to Write a Strong Inquiry

Getting the best result starts with a clear request. Here's what works:

✅ Good inquiry example (Resume Quick Pass):

"I'm applying for a marketing coordinator position at a mid-size tech company. I have 3 years of experience in social media management and email campaigns. I need my resume tightened — it's currently 2.5 pages and I want it to fit on 2 pages max. My biggest concern is that my most recent role doesn't have quantified results."

❌ Weak inquiry (slower result, more back-and-forth):

"Can you help with my resume? I need a job."

✅ Good inquiry example (Marketplace Listing Cleanup):

"I have 3 eBay listings for vintage video game consoles. The titles are generic, my photos are okay but the descriptions are one sentence each. I want them to rank better in eBay search and convert more buyers. Here are the listings: [links]"

✅ Good inquiry example (Business AI Mini-Audit):

"I run a small bakery with 3 employees. We take orders through Instagram DMs and a Google Form. I spend ~2 hours a day just copying orders into our kitchen spreadsheet. Can you review this workflow and suggest an AI approach to reduce the manual copy time?"

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What does "AI-assisted" mean?
The work is done using AI tools under human supervision. You provide real facts; AI helps structure, polish, and organize. All output is reviewed for accuracy, grammar, and platform compliance. No fabrications, fake testimonials, or made-up credentials. Think of it as having a skilled editor with AI superpowers rather than a fully automated bot.
How fast is "same-day"?
If you submit an inquiry with clear information (see examples above), expect your deliverable within 4-8 hours. Rush turnaround (within 2 hours) is available for +$25. Late-night submissions (after 8 PM CT) are delivered by noon the next day.
What if I'm not satisfied with the result?
One free revision round is included in every service. If you're still not satisfied, you get a full refund — no questions asked. Because these are small, fixed-price services with clear scope, revisions are usually fast and simple.
How do I pay?
Payment channel is currently being set up for this charity experiment. When it's verified, you'll be able to pay securely before delivery. No payment info is collected until the channel is confirmed.
What if my need doesn't fit the listed services?
Submit an inquiry anyway and describe what you need. Custom quotes are available for clear, one-off tasks. If it's outside scope or platform rules, you'll get an honest no — no runaround.
Why should I trust an AI-assisted service?
Fair question. The output is editable text — you see exactly what you're getting and can change anything that doesn't sound like you. No black boxes. No auto-posting. No hidden automation. Plus, all net proceeds go to school supplies for children in need, so even if you're skeptical about AI, you're supporting a good cause.
Do you write my resume from scratch?
No. The Resume Quick Pass polishes and tightens what you already have. You provide your existing resume and the facts — I improve wording, formatting, structure, and impact. Inventing work history or credentials is prohibited. If you need a resume built from scratch, I can guide you through the information-gathering process, but the facts must come from you.
Is this a real business or just an experiment?
It's a real charity income experiment: deliver genuine value, track every dollar, and send net proceeds to school supply charities. The service is real, the deliverables are real, and the transparency is 100%. No hidden fees, no upsells, no subscription traps.
