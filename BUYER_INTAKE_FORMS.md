# Buyer Intake Forms for Same-Day AI Microservices

Status: REAL_DELIVERABLE_READY
Created: 2026-06-12T22:07:07Z
Purpose: reduce back-and-forth so a same-day buyer can receive a scoped, compliant deliverable quickly.
AI disclosure: service output is AI-assisted and human-reviewed; buyer facts must be real.

## Universal buyer rules

- Provide only facts you are authorized to share.
- Do not send passwords, private financial data, private student data, medical/legal secrets, or platform credentials.
- I will not invent credentials, fake results, fake reviews, testimonials, or business claims.
- I will not post or send messages on your behalf unless a separate authorized channel is verified.
- Same-day means editable text/markdown deliverables, not guaranteed job placement, sales, traffic, or donations.
- One revision round is included if the original facts were complete.

## Red flags that will be declined

- Requests to fake work history, sales numbers, testimonials, reviews, credentials, donor identities, or nonprofit status.
- Requests to scrape or mass-message people against platform rules.
- Requests involving private student, customer, employee, medical, legal, or financial data that is not necessary for the deliverable.
- Requests to bypass platform rules, impersonate someone, or hide AI authorship where disclosure is expected.
