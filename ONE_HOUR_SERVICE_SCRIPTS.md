# One-Hour Service Scripts

Use these scripts when a prospect needs a very small, fixed-scope task. They are designed to reduce friction while preserving honesty, scope, and same-day delivery.

Revenue rule for the experiment: these scripts are only `READY_FOR_LEE_TO_SEND`. They are not sent outreach, not buyer intent, and not revenue until a real buyer/payment record exists.

## $25 resume headline / summary cleanup

Hi [name] — I can do a quick $25 resume headline/summary cleanup today.

Deliverable: a tighter headline, 2-3 summary options, and a short list of wording fixes. You provide your current resume text and the job type you are targeting. I will not invent experience or credentials.

If you want it, send:
1. Current headline/summary
2. Target job title
3. 3 real strengths or accomplishments

Turnaround: same day after the details are complete.

## $50 two-listing marketplace mini-pack

## No-payment-channel fallback

If you still want to proceed, we can keep the request documented and move forward only once a secure, mutually acceptable payment path is confirmed.
